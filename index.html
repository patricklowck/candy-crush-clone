The line

    How to play

    Swipe or tap two side-by-side pieces to swap them. Line up 3 or more of a kind.

    Match 4 to make a Line Blaster. It clears its whole row or column.

    Match an L or T to make a Bomb. It clears the block around it.

    Match 5 in a row to make a Red Dot. Swap it with any piece to clear that whole type.

    Construction lots get built when you match on them. Hazard-striped lots need two matches.

    Boosters: Hammer smashes one piece, Shuffle remixes the board, +3 Moves buys time. Three stars earn more.
